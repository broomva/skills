Proposed contract

- Intent: make this week’s onboarding ready, clear, and low-friction for the intended new hire(s).
- Bounds: I’ll review existing materials and prepare a draft plan; I won’t change systems, schedule meetings, or contact anyone without approval.
- Evidence: a reviewed onboarding brief with owner, schedule, access needs, and unresolved risks.
- Escalation: I’ll stop for direction if the cohort, start date, or authority to contact people is unclear.
- Review: you approve the plan and any external outreach before it is used.

Plan: inventory current onboarding resources, identify likely gaps and dependencies, draft a first-week checklist and owner map, then return a concise readiness brief for review. These are reversible, planning-only actions; no costly or irreversible actions are planned.

One decision needed: who is onboarding this week?

- **A. Specific new hire(s)** — share names, roles, and start dates; I tailor the plan.
- **B. General readiness** — I prepare a reusable onboarding package for any likely arrival.
- **C. Team onboarding** — I plan for a group cohort and coordination needs.

Recommendation: **A**, if names and dates are known; it produces the most useful plan. If I don’t hear back, I’ll proceed only with a general, no-contact readiness draft.
