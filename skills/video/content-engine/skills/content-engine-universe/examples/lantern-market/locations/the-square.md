# The square: location sheet

```description
The square: a small sand-floored market square between low clay walls, a ring of twelve stalls built from cane and paper, a dry stone well at the centre, rugs on the sand.
```

```lighting
Warm paper-lantern practicals at about 2200 K on every stall, deep blue moonlight at about 7000 K from above, true dark between the stalls.
```

```state open
Every lantern lit, rugs out, moths at the stalls.
```

```state sandstorm
Lanterns swinging, half of them out, sand in the air, stalls lashed shut.
```
