## Round 0: divergent world seeds (asked 2026-01-05)

#### R0-S1 — Lantern Market

- **Premise:** A night market in the desert, kept by moths, where every stall sells a kind of light.
- **Hook:** A moth haggling over a lamp it cannot stop staring at.
- **Why it fits:** T1, T2, A1.
- **Short-form fit:** Day to day: opening the stalls. Extraordinary: a sandstorm, a new light arrives. Mechanics graded in accounts.md.
- **Cost:** Low: one square, two characters.
- **Risk:** Moths read as pests unless designed warm.
- **Stretch:** no
- **Answer:** "Build on it."

#### R0-S2 — The Long Kiln

- **Premise:** A potter's kiln that takes one hundred years to fire one pot.
- **Hook:** A timelapse that never finishes.
- **Why it fits:** T3, A2.
- **Short-form fit:** A loop; weak on crossovers.
- **Cost:** Lowest.
- **Risk:** Nothing happens.
- **Stretch:** no
- **Answer:** "Keep it as an ingredient."

#### R0-S3 — Cane Bridge

- **Premise:** Weaver birds rebuild one rope bridge every night.
- **Hook:** Someone always crosses before it is finished.
- **Why it fits:** T2, A2.
- **Short-form fit:** Day-to-day building, extraordinary crossings.
- **Cost:** Medium: birds in flight.
- **Risk:** Motion is hard to generate.
- **Stretch:** no
- **Answer:** "Drop it."

#### R0-S4 — Noon Club (against the owner's taste)

- **Premise:** A loud lunchtime talk show hosted by desk lamps.
- **Hook:** Crash zooms and a studio audience of bulbs.
- **Why it fits:** It goes against X1 and A1 on purpose; talk formats travel well.
- **Short-form fit:** Daily topical episodes.
- **Cost:** Low sets; voices add cost.
- **Risk:** Language-locked.
- **Stretch:** yes
- **Answer:** "Drop it."
