# Lantern Market: bible

## Premise

A desert night market kept by moths, where every stall sells a kind of light. [`world-premise`]

## Tone, genre, rating and audience

- **Tone:** quiet, warm, patient.
- **Genre:** slice of life with small wonders.
- **Rating:** all ages.
- **Audience:** anyone who likes small lights in the dark.

## World rules

| Rule | Statement | Canon id |
|---|---|---|
| Economy | Light is the only currency | `rule-light-currency` |

## Two modes

- **Day-to-day:** opening the stalls, haggling, closing at dawn.
- **Extraordinary:** a sandstorm; a new kind of light arrives.

## What this world is not

- Not neon, not chrome, no screens.
- No real person's likeness; no other studio's characters.
