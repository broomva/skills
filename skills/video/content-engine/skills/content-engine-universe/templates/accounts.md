## How to read the evidence grades

| Grade | Means | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| measured | counted on real posts, with a method and a sample | build on it |
| reported | a credible source states it; not measured here | build on it, and watch it |
| heuristic | a low-confidence pattern (a keyword count, a few examples) | test it before relying on it |
| proposal | someone's idea; no evidence yet | a bet; say so to the owner |
