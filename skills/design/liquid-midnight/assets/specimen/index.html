06 · Motion

Springs, sweeps, one clock.

  • --ua 40°/s around every rim; focal rings 2×.
  • Springs: response .5, damping 1 default; scene .34; thumb .4; panel .6; playful .8/.9.
  • Press: scale(.96); chips .97; no CSS transitions on buttons.
  • Metal sweep: one pass ≈ 6.2 s per path, seeded offsets.
  • Ambient blobs drift 44/56/60 s, blur 90px.
  • ASCII flow: alpha .15 night / .12 day, cell 15, glyph 11.
  • Entrances ease-out cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1); exits ≈45% shorter.
  • renderAt(t): fixed 1/60 s steps from 0; seeded PRNG.
