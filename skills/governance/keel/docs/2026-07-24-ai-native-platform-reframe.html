AI-native platform: from rejection to constructive grounding layer

ADR · 2026-07-24 · Accepted · supersedes the originating design session’s §2.5

The originating design session rejected the "AI-native platform" framing outright. That rejection was correct about the failure mode and too broad about the remedy. This ADR narrows it: Keel may construct loop topology; it may not author anchors. The tonight build plan is unchanged.

Context

Handoff §2.5 rejected the originally-proposed platform — agentic workspaces reading a company's operations, source, docs and integrations (Composio et al.) to synthesize a workflow graph — as "the construction of a maximally ungrounded graph: agents verifying against artifacts that agents increasingly write."

The rejection rested on concept/grounded-vs-ungrounded-improvement.md and the conservation law in concept/evidentiary-independence-conservation.md. Both hold. But re-reading the first against the proposal exposes a misread.

The misread

grounded-vs-ungrounded-improvement.md:58-69 does not say "do not build the graph." It enumerates exactly three things the topology cannot supply:

  1. Anchors — "revenue that landed in the bank, tests that actually executed, customers who actually stayed"
  2. Frozen nodes — "rules the optimizing loops are never allowed to tune"
  3. The root definition of "better" — "supplied by people, through contact with real failures"

Everything else is presented as constructible. :51-56 lists the topological answers explicitly — pairing (every optimizing loop gets a counter-metric), hierarchy (a slower loop owns the faster loop's reference), arbitration (a loop above that owns the trade-off), and audit loops (whose only function is checking the other loops' numbers still touch the world).

§2.5 conflated build the graph with build the graph and let it author its own anchors. Only the second is fatal.

The enabling distinction

Independence cannot be manufactured, but it can be routed.

In any real company the exogenous signals already exist and are simply not wired to anything: invoices settle, shipments arrive, customers renew or churn. An agent that discovers those producers and rewires a check to read them is not creating independence — it is connecting independence that already existed. The conservation law is not violated, because conserved quantities can still be routed.

This is also the form the research demands. evidentiary-independence-conservation.md:363-368 moves the predicate from an audit-time proof obligation to a write-time admission predicate — reject the node whose evidence fork-point sits above the actor's write boundary. Build-time, not bolted on afterwards.

Decision

✓ Keel constructs

  • Counter-metric pairings for each optimizing loop
  • The hierarchy — which loop owns which reference
  • Arbitration loops where loops conflict
  • Audit loops re-checking that numbers touch the world
  • The routing of existing anchors into unanchored checks

✗ Keel imports, never authors

  • Anchors — discovered in existing operations, never invented
  • Frozen nodes — pre-committed; the agent may never tune the anchor registry
  • The root "better" — human, once, per company

Composio's role is corrected. Not a competitor and not the graph builder — the anchor-discovery layer. It answers one bounded question: which exogenous producers does this company already own? That is the one input a repo scan can never provide. Verified reachable today: composio search "payment settled webhook" returns STRIPE_GET_EVENTS (CLI v0.2.31 installed; stripe connection present but EXPIRED).

The binding constraint on construction

The constructing loop may not be the scoring loop. verifier-independence-depletes-under-optimization.md:44-50 — the moment the score becomes a selection signal, "you hill-climb the generator straight into the region where the two agree, including where they are jointly wrong." Freezing does not save it (:97-108): "freeze stops the gradient; only a query budget stops the selection."

This is not hypothetical for our case. self-improvement-verifier-ceiling.md:59-64 cites Self-Harness (arXiv:2606.09498): agents that autonomously edit their own scaffolding converge in 3–5 iterations and fix only behavioural errors. "The skill builds its own loop graph and grades it" is harness-axis self-improvement, and it plateaus — with the ratio still rising, which is worse than plateauing visibly.

The cadence separation has numbers rather than a vibe — the RCS λ margins (handoff §2.3): L1 0.411 → L2 0.069 → L3 0.006, roughly 6× then 11×. The ε-audit agreement rate is the depletion detector: it was designed as a counter-metric on the probe library and turns out to be exactly the instrument this architecture requires.

Why this is strong rather than merely permitted

The two structures Keel would construct — pairing and arbitration — are precisely the two gaps this workspace's own research found in bstack. Handoff §2.4, verified by grep: zero hits for counter-metric/metric pairing, zero hits for arbitration/conflicting loops. Filed as Gap 1 and Gap 2, unresolved.

So the product emits the structures its own governance was measured to lack: the counter-metric for P20's unpaired 7/10 and P6's unpaired Nous ≥ 5/9, and the arbitration cell for the m6-vs-P16 conflict that §2.4 records being settled by "ad-hoc agent judgment." Dogfood, generality proof, and the answer to "does this work outside a toy" in one move.

Consequences

The gatherer cannot do this: of 8 declared NodeKinds, only 4 are reachable — integration, deploy_gate and doc_claim are declared and never emitted (gather.ts:191-200, 229-234). By Keel's own predicate that claim is a self_referential doc_claim inside Keel. Resolve before pitching by either implementing one integration node or narrowing the sentence. Left open deliberately — it is a product decision, not a documentation one.
