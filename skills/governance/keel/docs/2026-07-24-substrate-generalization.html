Keel beyond the repo — substrate generalization

Design · 2026-07-24 · Roadmap — not scheduled

Keel's predicate needs exactly one thing: an actor with a definable write boundary. Source code is the easiest substrate to demonstrate it on, not the only one it fits — and a public company is arguably a cleaner fit than a repo.

The operator, stated once

An assay is a measurement whose provenance forks above the generator's write boundary.

Already stated in the originating design session. Everything below is that one sentence applied to three substrates.

SubstrateThe actorAnchored producersGather is…
A repo the agent / author process exit codes, type checkers, third-party probers mechanical — parse CI configs
Company operations internal agents + staff payments settled, shipments delivered, customers renewed integration enumeration (Composio et al.)
A public company management cash collected, regulators, exchanges, restatements extraction over prose — the hard one

Why the public-company fit is unusually clean

The actor/producer split is sharper here than in a repo, because securities law has already drawn the line for us:

Management CAN writeManagement CANNOT write
guidance · non-GAAP definitions · accounting estimates · MD&A narrative · "adjusted EBITDA" · related-party structuring · revenue recognition timing cash that settled at a bank · SEC enforcement actions · exchange prices · customer renewals · short-seller findings · restatements · auditor resignation

Note what falls out for free: the gap between recognized revenue (management-authored judgment) and cash collected (anchored) is the accruals question — Keel's predicate rediscovers earnings quality from first principles rather than importing it.

The finding worth the whole exercise

A standard audit opinion looks anchored and is not — conditionally. The company selects and pays its own auditor. From concept/evidentiary-independence-conservation.md:

a third-party witness only adds independence if the releasing authority can't choose which witness signs — pre-commit the witness-selection function before the incident window, else it degrades to Waiting-Room-for-a-Friendly-Clock

Arthur Andersen/Enron and EY/Wirecard are that failure mode. So the correct verdict on an unqualified opinion is conditionally anchored — real only insofar as the selection function is independent — and the writeBoundary.argument must carry that condition explicitly. This falls directly out of the existing predicate. No new theory required.

Validation: backtest against known outcomes

The repo corpus has no answer key. This substrate does. Run the predicate on pre-collapse filings only and check whether the grounding ratio was already low before the fraud surfaced.

Verified reachable (SEC EDGAR, free, no auth beyond a User-Agent header) — Luckin Coffee, CIK 0001767582:

  • 86 pre-collapse filings retrievable (< 2020-07)
  • IPO prospectus 424B4 — 2019-05-17
  • Secondary offering 424B4 — 2020-01-10, filed three weeks before the Muddy Waters report, while the fabricated sales were live

That January 2020 prospectus is a document management filed at the peak of the fraud. A low score on it, computed from public text alone, is the most persuasive number this project can produce — and any audience understands it without a software background.

Honest caveats

  1. This is a mature field. Earnings quality and forensic accounting have eighty years behind them — accruals ratio, Beneish M-score, Dechow F-score. "Is the signal independent of management" is the definition of auditing. Do not claim invention. The novelty is one operator applied uniformly across substrates, which is what §2.6 already frames.
  2. Gather stops being mechanical. In a repo it parses YAML. In a 10-K it is extraction over prose — lower precision, and the discipline that keeps Keel honest (carry the literal snippet, never a summary) gets materially harder to honor. This is the real engineering cost and it is not small.
  3. Out of scope for now. No near-term window, and it competes with work that de-risks the demo actually being given.

What this costs in the schema

Almost nothing, which is the encouraging part. The two repo-locked fields are metadata, not predicate — Node.source ("repo-relative path", keel.ts:33) and Report.revision ("commit sha", keel.ts:149). Widening both to generic provenance strings (a filing accession number, an integration event id) is a comment change. NodeKind already reserves doc_claim and integration, neither of which the gatherer currently emits.

Disposition
