Keel · design system

Never let the artifact make a claim it cannot show the evidence for.

Oasis's rigor, Keel's register. The structure — layered tokens, specimen cards, an agent-facing component brief, a machine-checkable adherence gate — is adapted from the Oasis Design System. The look is inverted on purpose: Oasis sells calm, Keel reports that your pipeline is green and nothing has been verified.

Read the standards — seven principles, voice, foundations, enforcement, and the constraints that produced them.
