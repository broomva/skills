A faceted keel blade cutting down through a lit waterline: the pale portion rides above the surface, the luminous portion is the part doing the structural work below it, where you cannot see it. The mark carries its own near-black canvas, so it composites onto surfaces Keel does not control without needing a paper variant.

Rules. Below 32px use mark-compact.png — a zoomed crop, not a scaled copy, because the full composition thins to a sliver. Clear space is half the mark's height on every side. Place it only on dark surfaces; it has no light variant and its glow disappears on paper. Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or re-render it at a different angle.

Why this breaks the surface rules. The mark uses glow, gradient, and faceted shading, all of which keel.css forbids on UI surfaces. That is deliberate and bounded: the emblem is a rendered object, the interface is not. Nothing in the product chrome may borrow this treatment — the moment a card or a button grows a glow, the exception has leaked.
